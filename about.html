03 : Behind the Code

Turning Curiosity Into Code

I have always loved finding order in the middle of chaos. My journey started in Business Administration, where I spent my time studying data workflows and the ways systems connect. But I soon found myself looking past the spreadsheets, I wanted to know how the machinery worked underneath.

That curiosity led me to dive deep into technology. I taught myself Python, SQL, Advanced Excel, Development, and Git/GitHub, falling in love with the quiet power of writing a script, managing version control, or structuring a database to take something complicated and make it simple, clean, and automated. I have since been able to apply these skills in a professional setting, helping businesses streamline their data operations and make better decisions based on the insights they uncover.

The Art of the "Proof-First" Mindset

I believe the best way to show who you are is through the things you create. I don't just write code to solve problems; I build whole frameworks to understand them completely

My favorite creation so far is my Technical Handbook. Built using MkDocs, it’s a beautifully organized, bilingual space where I’ve documented everything I know about Excel, Python, GitHub, SQL, and HTML. To me, mastering these diverse tools and creating clear documentation are two sides of the same coin, it's all about making technical ecosystems feel welcoming, structured, and easy to navigate.

What's Next on the Horizon

I recently packed up my life and moved to Germany, starting a beautiful new chapter. I am continuously expanding my software architecture toolkit, diving into full-stack development, RESTful APIs, and building end-to-end data applications.

Right now, I am looking to step into a technical, data-driven role where I can fully utilize my skills. I want to bring my meticulous eye for detail, my love for clean data operations, and my passion for building automated systems to a tech team that values creativity, continuous learning, and thoughtful collaboration.
